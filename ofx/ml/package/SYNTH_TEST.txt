SCENE 1  gaps   (tests Smart Fill, B3)
  Files: gaps_rgb.exr, gaps_matte.exr, gaps_truth.exr, gaps_label.exr (grey = label/3: black bg, dark grey subject,
  light grey = defect hole to FILL, white = real gap to KEEP).
  The matte has 10 defect holes (47 to 9902 px, some clear, some grey) and 5 REAL gaps (arm on hip 20441 px, between
  the legs 9136, a ring in the hand 4049, a small ring 696, an earring 193), sizes overlap on purpose, plus a grey wash on
  the right side of the torso. A warm striped subject on a cool background (colour makes it easy: it is a plumbing test).
  Expect with Smart Fill ON (defaults: threshold 0.6, Max Hole 10 %, Lite):
    View = Holes: all 10 defect holes GREEN (filled), all 5 real gaps RED (kept); the result matches gaps_truth.
    (Core evaluation: 196/196 defects filled, 100/100 real gaps kept, the real BiRefNet reproduces it.)
  Compare: plugin 1 Fill Holes with Max Hole 1 % fills the arm gap and part of the others (40 % of real-gap pixels
  wrongly), Max Hole 0.2 % leaves most defects unfilled: size alone cannot separate them.
  Try: Subject Threshold 0.3 / 0.9, Max Hole % 0.5, Segmentation Full; wash is NOT a hole (left alone).

SCENE 2  hair_flat, hair_busy   (tests Edge Refine on hair; B1)
  Files: hair_flat_rgb / _matte / _truth.exr and the same for hair_busy (busy textured background).
  The truth has 260 thin hair strands (1 to 3 px) on top of the head; the matte is the coarse version with the strands lost.
  Expect with Edge Refine ON (Band px 10, Mix 1): the head contour is cleaned, but most strands stay lost
  (about 13-18 % of the lost strand alpha comes back over the whole frame; about 80 % of it lies outside even a 30 px band).
  Try Band px 10 / 30 / 60: more strands back, slower. View = Changes shows what the model changed.
  NEW: the Edge Refine control "Outer px" (default 0) reaches further OUT on the background side, where the lost strands are.
  Try Band px 10 with Outer px 100 and 140: expect about 5x more strand alpha back (core numbers: 0.13 -> 0.67 on hair_flat / hair_busy)
  and about +25 % time. Above about 190 px it starts to spill on the busy background. On the clean scenes Outer px costs quality: leave it 0.
