FLAME TEST FOR MATTE MERGE 0.1.0 (plugin 3)
===========================================
Written 2026-10-24. About 45 minutes. On akflame5. Nothing here has been seen in Flame yet: every item is
a first look.

WHAT THIS TESTS
  Matte Merge (ofx/merge/CLAUDE.md, decision 37): a base matte (or trimap) plus a detail matte in, one
  combined matte out. The base keeps the subject and body edge; the detail matte (e.g. LTX Alpha Gen)
  adds hair / fine edge detail near the base's edge only, aligned to the picture first.
  Checked here: the sockets (the probe's "mask" layout), the footers, each View, each control, speed,
  proxy, Batch Render, colour tags, logging and Collect Logs.

SETUP (assistant builds; you do the sudo + restart)
  1. sudo ln -s ~/MatteRefine_build/<build>/MatteMerge.ofx.bundle /usr/OFX/Plugins/
     (the assistant gives the exact folder). Restart Flame.
  2. The node is "Matte Merge" (group "Matte Refine"). One tab, five columns:
       Matte (View, Version) · Zone (Source, Reach % of height, Keep Disconnected, Bridge % of height) ·
       Align (Align) ·
       Combine (Method, Detail Width % of height, Strength, Feather % of height) ·
       Support (Picture Evidence, Debug Logging, Collect Logs)
     Note if Flame lays it out differently (a second tab, an empty column, cut labels, doubles shown as
     whole numbers).
  3. Turn Debug Logging on (Support column): one DEBUG line per render. Log:
       ~/.cache/matte-refine/logs/matte-refine.log   (lines with "node=mm:<id>"; load lines "node=mm")
     Follow it live:  tail -f ~/.cache/matte-refine/logs/matte-refine.log | grep "mm"

FOOTAGE
  P = the picture (e.g. the Log3G10 kitchen clip)
  B = a base matte for P (roto, a keyer or a guided model: good body edge, soft or missing hair)
  D = a detail matte for P (LTX Alpha Gen: good hair, maybe offset / scaled / extra blobs)
  T = optional: a trimap for P (black outside, white inside, grey band at the edge)

CONNECT (the probe's layout: one red socket, two blue)
  P -> the front (red) socket, B -> the first blue socket (source alpha), D -> the second blue socket
  ("Detail"). Note if the sockets look different from the probe's mask variant.

TESTS
  1. Version label: "0.1.0 (<git>)". Log has "==== Matte Merge 0.1.0 (<git>) load (describe) ====".
  2. Footers, one at a time:
     a. Only P connected (no B, no D): "The source alpha is constant 1.00: connect the base matte or trimap
        to the node's second socket (source alpha)." Output = the input, alpha 1.
     b. P + B, no D: "Connect the detail matte to the Detail input." Alpha = B exactly.
     c. P + B + D: an info line, e.g. "Align: picture: move + scale, +-0.12 px. Detail: 12.3k px kept,
        1.2k dropped." Write it down for a few frames. "Align: no alignment (could not register)" means
        the detail is used as it arrives.
     d. Disconnect D again: back to b. Reconnect: back to c.
     Say whether the info footer (not a warning) shows at all, and whether it stays when scrubbing
     (it is only re-sent when its text changes).
  3. Views (Matte column), with Method Add Fine Detail, all defaults:
     Result Matte  RGB = the combined matte (should equal the matte output). Does it look like B plus D's hair?
     Picture       RGB = P. Is it P as it looks in the input (raw), or converted? (With D connected Flame
                   converted the picture in the probe; this is expected, note what you see.)
     Added         what D added (white; green would mean the base was lowered: only with Zone Blend).
                   Hair only? Any detail floating away from the head (= misaligned)?
     Rejected      detail that was dropped (blobs, other objects, strands not connected to the subject).
     Zone          where the detail may act (a band around B's edge).
     Residual      where D's thick body still differs from B after alignment (bright = misaligned or
                   a different shape).
     For each: is the image in the viewer greyscale and not tone-mapped (colour tag "Matte"), and Picture
     shown as a normal picture (tag = the source's)? A grey-looking matte means the tag was not applied.
  4. Controls (View Result Matte or Added):
     Reach % of height  2 / 6 / 15: the zone grows; does long hair come back at 15? Slower?
     Bridge % of height 0 / 0.5 / 1.5 (Add Fine Detail, Matte Edge): at 0 is there a dark gap between the
                        head and the strands? At 0.5 do the strands join the head? At 1.5 does the head get
                        visibly fatter (the detail matte's body edge taken over)? DEBUG line "bridge=".
     Detail Width       0.25 / 0.5 / 1.5: thicker strands counted as detail at higher values.
     Strength           0 (= B) / 0.5 / 1.
     Feather            0 (hard border at the zone's edge, look for a visible line) / 0.25 / 1.
     Align              Off vs Auto: with Off, does the hair sit off the head? Footer "Align: off."
     Method             Zone Blend: the detail replaces the base in the zone (the body edge may change).
     Picture Evidence   On: fewer invented strands? Real ones lost? Compare with View Rejected.
  5. Trimap: put T on the source alpha instead of B, Source = Trimap. The grey band is filled from D.
     Keep Disconnected on: loose strands and blobs stay. Note what happens outside the grey band (it
     should stay exactly black / white).
  6. Speed: scrub 20 frames at full resolution; note whether it feels interactive. The DEBUG line has
     "read / merge / write / render ... ms": send a few. Same at UHD if you have it.
  7. Proxy mode (half resolution): the result should look the same as at full resolution (sizes are a %
     of the height). DEBUG line "scale=0.5" and the frame size.
  8. Batch Render 20 frames with D connected: no error, output alpha = the Result Matte.
  9. Two Matte Merge nodes in one batch, different settings: each keeps its own.
 10. Save the batch, reload: settings kept, View kept, Debug Logging follows the per-user setting.
 11. Collect Logs: popup "Support bundle written to ~/matte-refine-support-<time>.tar.gz".

WHAT TO TELL THE ASSISTANT
  - the layout (tabs, columns, any cut labels), the sockets;
  - the footers of test 2 (exact text);
  - per View: what it shows, and whether the colour tag looked right;
  - per control: the effect and anything wrong (hair floating off the head, a hard line, flicker);
  - the DEBUG render lines for a few frames (they hold the align model, uncertainty, move, zone and
    kept / dropped counts and the timings: no image data), or just press Collect Logs and send the bundle.

AFTERWARDS
  Keep the link for the next round, or: sudo rm /usr/OFX/Plugins/MatteMerge.ofx.bundle and restart Flame.
