MATTE REFINE ML FOR FLAME - THIRD-PARTY MODELS AND LIBRARIES
============================================================

Matte Refine ML runs machine-learning models on your GPU. The models and the NVIDIA libraries are
NOT part of the plugin: setup.sh downloads them to your machine, from the sources below, for your
use under their own licences. By accepting you confirm that you have read these terms.
(A summary, not legal advice; the licence texts govern.)

1. ViTMatte (Edge Refine): the "Small" model, and "Base" if you choose the full models
   What:     ViTMatte image matting model trained on Composition-1k, ONNX conversion
   Licence:  MIT, "Copyright (c) 2023 Hust Vision Lab" (text below)
   Source:   https://github.com/hustvl/ViTMatte (code and licence)
             https://huggingface.co/hustvl/vitmatte-small-composition-1k (weights)
             downloaded from https://huggingface.co/Xenova/vitmatte-small-composition-1k
             and https://huggingface.co/Xenova/vitmatte-base-composition-1k (ONNX files)
   Size:     Small 104 MB, Base 387 MB
   Training data: the Composition-1k dataset from Deep Image Matting:
             Ning Xu, Brian Price, Scott Cohen, Thomas Huang. "Deep Image Matting."
             CVPR 2017. https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.03872

2. BiRefNet (Smart Fill): the "Lite" model, and the full model if you choose the full models
   What:     BiRefNet segmentation model (subject / background), ONNX conversion
   Licence:  MIT, "Copyright (c) 2024 ZhengPeng" (text below)
   Source:   https://github.com/ZhengPeng7/BiRefNet (code and licence)
             downloaded from https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/BiRefNet_lite-ONNX
             and https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/BiRefNet-ONNX (ONNX files)
   Size:     Lite 224 MB, full 973 MB

3. ONNX Runtime 1.31.0 (runs the models; shipped inside the plugin, not downloaded)
   What:     Microsoft's inference runtime with its CUDA execution provider
   Licence:  MIT, "Copyright (c) Microsoft Corporation" (text below); third-party notices in
             THIRD_PARTY/onnxruntime-ThirdPartyNotices.txt in the release package
   Source:   https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime/releases/tag/v1.31.0
   Size:     317 MB (inside the plugin)

4. NVIDIA CUDA 13 and cuDNN 9 runtime libraries (run the models on the GPU)
   What:     cuBLAS 13.8.1.7, CUDA runtime 13.4.92, cuRAND 10.4.4.72, cuDNN 9.27.0.42 (cu13):
             11 libraries taken from NVIDIA's Python packages
   Licence:  NVIDIA's own terms, not open source: the NVIDIA CUDA Toolkit End User License
             Agreement (cuBLAS, CUDA runtime, cuRAND) and the License Agreement for NVIDIA
             Software Development Kits (cuDNN). setup.sh saves the full texts, as shipped in
             NVIDIA's packages, next to the libraries (cuda_libs/licences/).
   Source:   https://pypi.org/project/nvidia-cublas/13.8.1.7/
             https://pypi.org/project/nvidia-cuda-runtime/13.4.92/
             https://pypi.org/project/nvidia-curand/10.4.4.72/
             https://pypi.org/project/nvidia-cudnn-cu13/9.27.0.42/
             NVIDIA's current terms online: https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/eula/index.html
             and https://docs.nvidia.com/deeplearning/cudnn/latest/reference/eula.html
   Size:     1.5 GB installed (1.0 GB download)

Every file is checked against a fixed SHA-256 before it is used.
